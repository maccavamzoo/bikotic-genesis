About Bikotic

Welcome to Bikotic, the premier visual comparison web app designed for bike enthusiasts interested in road bikes, mountain bikes, hybrid bikes, electric bikes, BMX bikes, gravel bikes, touring bikes, and triathlon bikes. Bikotic offers a unique service that allows you to visually compare scaled and aligned bikes from all these diverse categories. This innovative feature enables you to quickly discern the differences in geometry and specifications between the various types of bikes.

Extensive Search and Information

Bikotic's extensive search page is your gateway to exploring and comparing bikes with ease. The platform offers a plethora of useful information to ensure that you make an informed decision, whether you're a casual rider or a cycling aficionado.

Bikotic YouTube Channel

Bikotic also runs a popular YouTube channel where the latest bikes are reviewed. With nearly 4 million views since Bikotic's inception in 2017, it's a trusted resource in the cycling community. Discover more on Bikotic's YouTube channel for comprehensive bike reviews.
