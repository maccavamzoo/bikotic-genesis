Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10/12/2023

Bikotic.com DOES NOT:

  • Collect or store any personal information (unless you enter your email for a reply to a query)

Bikotic.com DOES:

  • Assign a session ID when you log on to the site, it then stores the date and the id of each bike viewed by this session ID. This information is completely anonymous, e.g session id: 432764 bike_ids: 1,234,678,345,23 - This information is stored so I can see which bikes are most popular at any given time.
  • Use Google ads

Cookie Usage

bikotic.com does not use cookie's, however Google ads does use cookies. Cookies are used by Google to display ads relevant to you. Please see Goolge's information for further details. Apart from this, bikotic.com does not use cookies for any other purpose, nor do we track or collect personal data through any other means.
